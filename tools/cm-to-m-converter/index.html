How to convert Centimeters to Meters?

The metric system makes length conversion straightforward because it uses powers of ten. Since "centi-" means one-hundredth, there are 100 centimeters in 1 meter.

Conversion Formulas:

  • To convert cm to m: Divide the length value by 100 (e.g., 50cm / 100 = 0.5m).
  • To convert m to cm: Multiply the length value by 100 (e.g., 2m × 100 = 200cm).
