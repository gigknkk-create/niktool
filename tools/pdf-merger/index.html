    How to merge PDF files online

    1. Select two or more PDF documents.
    2. Use the arrow buttons to put the files in the right order.
    3. Select Merge PDFs to create and download one combined file.

    Private, browser-based PDF merging

    The merge happens locally in your browser. Your documents are not sent to a server, and the finished PDF is downloaded directly to your device.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I merge PDF files?

    Choose two or more PDF files, arrange them in the order you want, and select Merge PDFs. The combined document will download when it is ready.

    Are my PDF files uploaded?

    No. Your files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to NikTool.

    Can I change the order of the PDFs?

    Yes. Use the Move up and Move down buttons beside each file before merging.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Password-protected or encrypted PDFs must be unlocked before they can be merged with this tool.
