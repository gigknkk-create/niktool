How to remove the last two pages of a PDF

  1. Upload your PDF file using the selection control above.
  2. Click Remove Last 2 Pages to process the document instantly.
  3. Download the trimmed PDF file directly to your device.

Why strip trailing pages?

Many downloaded PDFs come with generic back covers, blank end-matter, or trailing terms and conditions pages. Trimming the final two pages cleans up your document for printing or sharing.

Private browser-based processing

Your PDF is modified using WebAssembly and client-side JavaScript. Content never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum page count required?

The PDF must contain at least 3 pages so that at least 1 page remains after deleting the last two.

Are encrypted or password-protected PDFs supported?

You must remove password protection or unlock the PDF file before processing it.
