How to convert text case

  1. Type or paste your text into the input box.
  2. Choose the capitalization or naming style you need.
  3. Copy the converted result and use it anywhere.

Supported text styles

Use uppercase or lowercase for regular writing, title case for headings, sentence case for readable paragraphs, and camelCase, snake_case, or kebab-case for code, filenames, and URLs.

Private browser-based conversion

Your text stays on your device. NikTool processes it locally in the browser and does not send it to a server.

Frequently asked questions

What text cases can I generate?

You can generate uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case text.

Is my text uploaded?

No. All text conversion happens locally in your browser.

Can I use this tool for code variable names?

Yes. camelCase and snake_case are useful for variables, while kebab-case is useful for URLs and filenames.
