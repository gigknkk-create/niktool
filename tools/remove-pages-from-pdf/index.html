How to remove custom pages from a PDF

  1. Upload your PDF file using the selector above.
  2. Enter the pages or range to delete into the text field (for example, 2, 4-6, last 2).
  3. Click Remove Selected Pages and download your processed file.

Supported removal formats

This flexible tool accepts several input patterns combined with commas:

  • Individual pages: 2, 4, 7
  • Page ranges: 3-6 or 10-15
  • First N pages: first 2 or first 5
  • Last N pages: last 3 or last 1

Private browser-based processing

Your documents stay strictly on your device. All parsing and page manipulation run in client-side WebAssembly and JavaScript using pdf-lib.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine ranges and individual page numbers?

Yes! You can enter combinations like 1, 3-5, 8, last 2 in the page selection field.

Can I delete all pages in a document?

No, a PDF document must retain at least 1 remaining page to produce a valid file.
